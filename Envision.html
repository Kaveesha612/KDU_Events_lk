ENVISION 3.0



About ENVISION 3.0

ENVISION 3.0 is an annual, innovation-driven technical event organized by the IEEE Student Branch at KDU. It’s a three-day gathering that brings together bright and enthusiastic students, developers, strategists, and designers to ignite creativity and turn ideas into real impact.

ENVISION 3.0 is positioned as a launchpad for students (especially freshers) to explore their professional journey: it’s not just about winning it's about learning, collaborating, and building a strong foundation for their engineering careers.

The program features:

  • Debate sessions, where participants form teams and discuss relevant tech or social topics. and more.
  • Technical workshops led by industry experts, giving attendees hands-on learning in new tools and emerging technologies.
  • Hackathon competition, where teams build projects, solve real-world problems, and showcase their technical skills.
  • Talk sessions, sharing perspectives on the future of engineering, innovation, career pathways, and more.
