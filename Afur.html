A Gathering of Blessings

Afur '25



About Afur '25

The Tamil student body of General Sir John Kotelawala Defence University (KDU) organized a vibrant Pongal Celebration on the 5th of February 2025 at the KDU Soccer Ground, uniting students, faculty, and dignitaries to celebrate the harvest festival in splendour. The event beautifully captured the essence of Pongal, a festival dedicated to expressing gratitude for nature’s bounty. The students and faculty came together to celebrate Tamil heritage, fostering cultural appreciation and strengthening unity within the KDU community.


Event Details

🗓 Date: 5th of February 2025, 5:30 PM onwards

📍 Location: KDU Cafeteria, Ratmalana.
