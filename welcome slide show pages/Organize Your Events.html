Best Practices for Quality Listings ✨

A quality listing ensures maximum engagement and attendance for your event.

  • Clear Title: Keep it concise, catchy, and descriptive.
  • Date & Time: Specify start and end times clearly, including time zones if necessary.
  • Venue: Provide the exact location and any necessary directions or room numbers.
  • Poster/Image: Use a high-quality, non-busy visual (JPEG/PNG) with consistent styling.
  • Concise Description: Explain the event's purpose, target audience, and key highlights briefly.
  • Contact: Include a direct, accessible email or phone number for inquiries.
  • Accessibility Notes: Mention any accessibility accommodations (e.g., ramps, sign language).
