Rota Fiesta.

About Rota Fiesta

Rota Fiesta is an electrifying fusion of food, music, culture, and community, produced by the Rotaract Club of KDU with the mission of raising funds for meaningful community service initiatives.
This year’s theme “Feel the beat, Live the Fiesta” summons music lovers, foodies, creative spirits, and changemakers to unite under one sky and celebrate Sri Lanka’s vibrant energy.

Event Highlights

  • Live Performances & Music Acts
    Grooves by top bands and artists, including MidLane, Freeze Band, Wasthi, Piyath, Funky Dirt, Adithya, Channuka, Sana, Vinuja, Senal and more.
  • Food & Flavors
    Explore a diverse spread of culinary stalls featuring local and fusion cuisine the perfect companions to your evening of sound and celebration.
  • Interactive Zones & Games
    Get hands-on with creative spaces, photo booths, challenges, casual games, and fun activations for all ages.
  • Community & Cause
    Every ticket purchased supports community service projects facilitated by Rotaract KDU. Your presence amplifies impact.

Event Details
