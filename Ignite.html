IGNITE 2025



About IGNITE

IGNITE 2025 is one of the most electrifying and vibrant student events organized by the Faculty of Technology (SAFOT) of General Sir John Kotelawala Defence University. Designed to celebrate creativity, performance, and youthful energy, IGNITE brings together music, fashion, art, and culture into a single unforgettable night.

This year’s theme introduces “The Throne”, a powerhouse live band known for their unmatched rhythm, explosive energy, and commanding stage presence. IGNITE transforms the university cafeteria premises into a realm of fire, rhythm, and royalty where every beat, every performance, and every moment is crafted to captivate.

With live music, stunning fashion segments, dynamic stage productions, and a powerful atmosphere of unity and celebration, IGNITE 2025 promises an immersive experience for all attendees. The event embodies the spirit of creativity and student talent at KDU, offering a platform for performers to showcase their artistry while bringing the entire university community together for a night of excitement.

IGNITE is more than an event it is a celebration of passion, performance, and the vibrant cultural life of KDU.

Event Details

🗓 Date: 28th August 2025, 7:00 PM onwards

📍 Location: KDU Cafeteria, Ratmalana.
