International Research Conference 2025



About IRC

The International Research Conference (IRC) of General Sir John Kotelawala Defence University (KDU) is an annual multidisciplinary research forum that brings together academics, professionals, researchers, policymakers, and students from Sri Lanka and around the world. The conference provides a platform to share innovative ideas, present new research findings, and discuss emerging trends that contribute to national development and global knowledge.

18th International Research Conference KDU IRC 2025 is held under the theme “Bridging Frontiers: Interdisciplinary Research for Sustainable Progress”, emphasizing the importance of collaborative and cross-disciplinary research in addressing modern challenges. The conference includes multiple academic tracks, keynote speeches, technical sessions, and publication opportunities, continuing KDU’s commitment to fostering research excellence and intellectual exchange.

CONFERENCE TRACKS

  • Defence and Strategic Studies
  • Engineering
  • Management, Social Science & Humanities
  • Law
  • Built Environment & Spatial Sciences
  • Technology
  • Criminal Justice
  • Allied Health Sciences
  • Computing
  • Medicine
