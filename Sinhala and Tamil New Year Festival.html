KDU New Year Festival



About KDU New Year Festival

KDU Awurudu is the annual Sinhala and Tamil New Year festival celebrated with great cultural pride and unity at General Sir John Kotelawala Defence University. The event brings together Officer Cadets, Day Scholars, academic staff, and non-academic staff to experience the rich traditions of Sri Lankan heritage in a vibrant and festive atmosphere.

The celebration features a variety of traditional Awurudu games, including kana mutti bindeema, kotta pora, banis kema, adeeda geseema, kamba adeema and many more activities that highlight teamwork, joy, and friendly competition. Participants also take part in selecting the Awurudu Kumari and Awurudu Kumara, one of the most anticipated segments of the festival.

Beautifully decorated traditional huts (kamatha), cultural dancing, drumming, traditional costumes, and authentic Sri Lankan food stalls make the event lively and colorful. More than just a festival, KDU Awurudu strengthens camaraderie, cultural awareness, and unity among the university community upholding the spirit of the New Year in a uniquely KDU style.
