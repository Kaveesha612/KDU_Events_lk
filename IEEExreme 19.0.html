IEEExreme 19.0



About IEEExreme 19.0

IEEEXtreme 19.0 is the global, 24-hour programming competition organized by IEEE, where teams of up to three IEEE Student Members compete in real time to solve challenging algorithmic and coding problems.

This year’s edition will be held on 25 October 2025. IEEEXtreme Teams are supervised by proctors IEEE members who ensure compliance with the rules.

IEEEXtreme is more than a contest it's an intense, high-stakes challenge that tests your endurance, creativity, and technical ability. Participate to push your limits, represent KDU and take your place in a global community of coders.

Why You Should Join:

  • Teamwork & Collaboration:Participate in a team-based format to enhance problem-solving and coordination.
  • Global Exposure & Recognition:Compete with students from around the world, learn from peers, and build your programming portfolio.
  • Exciting Awards & Prizes:Top teams can win cash prizes, merchandise, and recognition from IEEE.
  • Participation Certificate:All teams that score at least one point will receive a certificate.IEEEXtreme
