About Yeh Jawaani

KDU Yeh Jawaani is a high-energy, youth-driven celebration of life, friendship, and adventure hosted by the students of General Sir John Kotelawala Defence University. Inspired by the spirit of wanderlust and unforgettable moments, this event invites students to let go, live freely, and embrace the thrill of youth.

Expect a dynamic evening filled with live music, candid performances, and heartfelt conversations. “Yeh Jawaani” is more than just a party — it’s a journey through memories, aspirations, and the bonds that bring the KDU community together. Whether you’re grooving with friends, reflecting on your journey so far, or looking ahead to what’s next, this event captures the essence of being young, bold, and unapologetically alive.
